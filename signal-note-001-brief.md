## Approved Sources

1. [Google Search Central — Optimizing Your Website for Generative AI Features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). The note uses Google’s current guidance that durable visibility depends on unique, helpful, people-first, non-commodity content and clear technical structure—not scaled pages built around every query variation.
2. [Google Search Central — Link Best Practices for Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable). The note uses Google’s guidance that crawlable, descriptive links help people and search systems understand and find related pages.

## Coreweaver Contribution

The Signal Note translates those official principles into one bounded operating decision: **treat a resource as a route between a reader question, one useful explanation, and the next relevant decision**. It does not restate the sources as a generic search checklist. It connects the principle to Coreweaver’s existing public architecture: Start Here routes, decision guides, Method, Research standards, and service relevance.

## Claim Boundary and Limitation

The source documents support guidance about useful, crawlable, well-linked content. They do **not** establish that a specific architecture will improve rankings, traffic, conversion, AI citations, leads, revenue, or any other commercial result. The Signal Note will make no such claim. It is an editorial operating interpretation, not original research or a performance study.

# Premium Starting-Price Architecture

## Approved Commercial Starting Prices

The figures below are **owner-approved commercial starting prices**. They are transparent entry points for a defined operating cadence, not all-inclusive promises or fixed prices for every scope. Final scope, channel mix, content volume, technical dependencies, sales enablement, reporting requirements, and ad spend remain subject to a documented statement of work.

| Launch service | Published starting price | Commercial structure | What is separate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| SEO | **From $6,500/month** | Approved strategic and operating starting price | Development implementation, third-party tools, localization, and unusually large content production |
| Content Marketing | **From $7,500/month** | Approved strategy, editorial, distribution, and measurement starting price | Large-format production, video, paid distribution, and subject-matter-expert access beyond agreed scope |
| Paid Ads | **From $7,500/month** | Approved paid-media strategy, campaign operations, creative coordination, and reporting starting price | **Media spend is client-funded and separate.** Any additional scope is documented in the statement of work. |

## Market Benchmark Context

Clutch’s 2026 [SEO pricing guide](https://clutch.co/seo-firms/pricing) reports a typical monthly SEO fee range of $2,000–$20,000, an average monthly project cost of $3,199.19, and common hourly rates of $100–$149. Its [Content Marketing pricing guide](https://clutch.co/agencies/content-marketing/pricing) reports typical agency rates of $100–$149 per hour and identifies strategy, research, content volume, distribution, visual production, localization, and target audience as material cost drivers. Its [PPC pricing guide](https://clutch.co/agencies/ppc/pricing) reports typical projects in the $10,000–$49,999 range, an average monthly project cost of $7,165.33, and common hourly rates of $100–$149; it also states that media spend and agency management fees are separate.

The Coreweaver Labs starting prices deliberately sit above undifferentiated execution pricing because they include senior operating judgment, cross-channel coordination, research, documented ownership, quality control, and measurement review. They should not be represented as evidence that a client will obtain specific rankings, traffic, leads, pipeline, revenue, or return on ad spend.

## Why the Premium Is Defensible

1. **Evidence before activity.** Each service starts with research, terminology, buyer context, and an inspectable baseline rather than a generic production calendar.
2. **Integrated operating system.** SEO, content, and paid media are planned as connected commercial functions, with named handoffs to sales and support.
3. **Senior review and governance.** The engagement includes transparent scope, documented decisions, claim-quality controls, and periodic measurement review.
4. **Mid-market complexity.** The offer is designed for B2B teams with longer consideration cycles, multiple stakeholders, and a need to explain specialist products clearly.
5. **No performance theater.** The pricing funds disciplined work; it does not buy fabricated case studies, artificial rankings, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Review Cadence

Review the starting-price architecture quarterly against actual delivery hours, margin, utilization, client fit, proposal win/loss reasons, and verified scope changes. Change published prices only after an owner-approved revision; preserve prior statements of work for existing clients.

# URL Dashboard Review — Evidence Boundary

**Source reviewed:** `URLDASHBOARD.pdf` supplied by the project owner  
**Review purpose:** Identify only supportable inputs for service positioning, market-presence planning, and future portfolio governance.

## What the Dashboard Contains

The document is a one-page inventory of URLs associated with Coreweaver, ARM, Arctura, and related internal properties. It includes owner-authored labels for a small number of properties, such as “working product,” “mid-funnel,” “top funnel,” and internal reference notes. It also includes an internal note about the present Coreweaver Labs site/hosting direction.

## Supportable Uses

| Finding | Permitted use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| The owner maintains a network of related web properties and product concepts | Internal service and information-architecture planning | The document alone does not establish public ownership, traffic, revenue, clients, or commercial results. |
| Some URLs have owner-authored funnel-role notes | Internal mapping of future content and service pathways | These notes are not independent evidence of actual funnel performance or conversion. |
| Coreweaver Labs has an identified primary web presence and a need for a modern service experience | Prioritize the Coreweaver Labs SSR site as the public premium-catalogue and evidence hub | Do not state that a prior platform or host failed without authorized, supportable public documentation. |
| Multiple internal tools or product URLs exist | Potential operational context for future research, design, and systems work | None may be presented as a client, portfolio project, case study, award, or Clutch evidence without owner confirmation and authorization. |

## Explicitly Unsupported by This Document

The dashboard does **not** substantiate any client relationship, case-study result, traffic level, conversion rate, ranking, revenue outcome, domain authority, review, Clutch eligibility, or third-party recognition. It must not be used as evidence for a premium claim, Clutch portfolio item, client testimonial, or performance promise.

## Required Authorization Before Public Use

Before naming any listed property in a public service page, portfolio, case study, Clutch profile, or review request, obtain confirmation of: legal control, brand relationship, current operational status, public naming permission, description of actual work performed, evidence source, scope, reporting period, and written publication authorization. The existing case-study intake and reviewer-handoff workflow is the required gate for any public proof record.

## Recommended Internal Action

Treat the dashboard as an internal architecture backlog. First, consolidate the Coreweaver Labs public experience around the premium SEO, Content Marketing, and Paid Ads catalogue. Then assess each related property individually for legitimate operational relevance. Do not aggregate the URLs into a public “portfolio” or infer commercial outcomes from their existence.

# Coreweaver Labs — Authorized Proof and Ethical Review Program

## Purpose and Non-Negotiables

This program turns **completed, authorized client work** into evidence that can be reviewed internally and, only when permitted, published or used in a Clutch profile. It never creates synthetic results, invented clients, anonymous “sample” testimonials, bought reviews, or feedback conditioned on a positive rating.

Clutch states that reviews, clients and experience, market presence, and service focus inform its provider assessment; the platform also describes identity checks and review screening. The implication is straightforward: evidence quality matters more than a manufactured volume target.[1] [2]

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| Invite a real client at a natural completion or value-realization milestone | Solicit a review before meaningful work is complete merely to boost a count |
| Ask neutrally for an honest account in the client’s own words | Script, ghostwrite, edit, buy, gate, or condition feedback on a favorable rating |
| Offer the same optional invitation process to eligible clients | Select only presumed-positive reviewers or exclude critical voices |
| Publish a case study only after source, privacy, claims, and written-authorization checks | Convert intake material, a dashboard, or a verbal approval into public evidence automatically |

## Evidence Pipeline

| Step | Private record | Required approval | Public outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Identify candidate | Internal delivery close-out note | Delivery owner confirms completion and client context | None |
| 2. Assemble evidence | Seven-field case-study intake: approved label, source, exact finding, scope, reporting window, review date, written authorization | Source owner and internal reviewer | None |
| 3. Review safety | Privacy, claims, and publication-handoff fields | Named reviewers and planned publication date | None |
| 4. Decide | Editorial decision log | Publish, revise, hold, or decline | Only a manually created, approved record may appear publicly |
| 5. Revalidate | Periodic source/date review | Evidence owner and editor | Update, qualify, archive, or retain |

## Neutral Review Invitation

Use this only for a real client after the appropriate milestone. Personalize factual references; do not insert suggested sentiment.

> **Subject:** Optional feedback on our recent work together  
> Thank you for working with Coreweaver Labs on **[approved scope]**. If you are comfortable sharing an honest account of your experience, communication, and the work’s context, you may use this independent review link: **[official review link]**. Your participation is entirely optional, and we value candid feedback. Please do not include confidential information.

The owner records the invite date, client contact, milestone, official review destination, and response status privately. No review text is copied into marketing materials without the reviewer’s permission and the relevant platform terms.

## Portfolio and Profile Readiness Checklist

Before creating or updating a Clutch profile, confirm every field below against source material.

| Profile component | Evidence required | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Legal company and location | Current legal/business information | Company owner |
| Services and allocation | Actual delivery capability for SEO, Content Marketing, and Paid Ads | Service owner |
| Team size, geography, industries | Current, supportable information | Operations owner |
| Portfolio item | Authorized client label, scope, method/source, outcome wording, disclosure limits | Evidence reviewer |
| Client review | Voluntary submission from a real client through the platform | Review-program owner |
| Public claim | Matching website statement and sourceable basis | Claims reviewer |

Clutch recommends detailed provider portfolios and says client/project context can strengthen provider profiles; Coreweaver Labs should treat this as a quality checklist rather than a quota to fill with unverified material.[3]

## Escalation and Audit

Any concern about privacy, accuracy, client authorization, platform terms, or a review request is a **stop-and-review** event. The designated owner pauses outreach or publication, preserves the source record, and asks the relevant privacy/claims/editorial reviewer for a written decision. Retain the decision with the private record.

# Coreweaver Labs — High-Value B2B Video Content System

**Status:** Approved production-system brief for the first Coreweaver cornerstone video. This document defines a useful public-information practice, not a performance forecast, platform guarantee, viewer-behavior claim, or automated publishing plan.

## First Principle

The first Coreweaver video should do **one decision-support job**: help a mid-market B2B team recognize why public information, evidence, and buyer language need to connect before a prospect is asked to trust a service claim. It should not attempt to explain every service, prove a result, predict buyer behavior, or use “AI visibility” as a promise.

The selected format is a **75–90 second, 16:9 cornerstone explainer** for the Coreweaver site and LinkedIn. A shorter vertical cutdown may be edited later from the approved master; it is not a separate claim or a reason to alter the original framing.

## Source-Supported Production Principles

LinkedIn’s B2B video guidance recommends planning for mobile viewing, using captions, maintaining a consistent aspect ratio within a video, and considering how a video can be extended into related formats. [1] Wistia’s 2026 report describes its analysis of more than 13 million videos and 79 million hours of viewing data; its editorial takeaways emphasize educational formats, a clear early message, contextual video placement, and an end-of-video next step. [2] These sources inform production choices, not a claim that a particular format will produce attention, engagement, leads, or revenue for Coreweaver.

| Principle | Coreweaver production rule | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **One viewer question** | Begin from the question: “How can a B2B team make its public information easier to inspect before a buyer conversation?” | Do not treat a question as proof of a universal buyer journey. |
| **Main idea early** | State the decision problem and Coreweaver’s bounded method in the opening 10–15 seconds. | This is an editorial clarity choice, not a retention guarantee. |
| **Educational value** | Teach a small visual model: unclear information → source/support check → connected public decision path. | Do not imply that the model creates rankings, citations, demand, pipeline, or revenue. |
| **Caption-first accessibility** | Produce accurate captions/transcript and maintain readable text contrast and visual labels. | Captions must be reviewed; generated captions are not accepted automatically. |
| **Consistent master frame** | Produce the primary video in 16:9. Derive a separate 9:16 cutdown only from approved footage and copy. | Do not switch framing inside the master video. |
| **Contextual CTA** | End with one visible, spoken next route: `/method` or the clarity-review conversation path. | The CTA offers a next step; it must not pressure, gate, or promise an outcome. |
| **Useful reuse** | Convert the approved master into a transcript, one LinkedIn post, an optional short cutdown, and relevant site-chat answers. | Reuse cannot add claims not present in the reviewed source script. |

## Cornerstone Video Brief

| Element | Approved default |
|---|---|
| **Working title** | *From unclear public information to a buyer-ready decision path* |
| **Primary viewer** | A mid-market B2B marketing, revenue, or operations lead who needs to make a complex public offer clearer before relying on more content, paid traffic, or AI tooling. |
| **Purpose** | Orient the viewer to Coreweaver’s evidence-led decision method and point toward a relevant public explanation. |
| **Length and master format** | 75–90 seconds in 16:9. |
| **Visual language** | Existing warm paper, deep ink, brass rules, restrained teal signal, simple evidence-card and route-map motion; no fabricated dashboards, customer logos, reviews, rankings, or results. |
| **Narration** | Calm, clear, off-screen narration with matching on-screen captions. |
| **Music** | Restrained ambient instrumental bed, mixed below narration and removable without losing meaning. |
| **CTA** | “See the method” linking to `/method`, with a secondary optional clarity-review conversation path. |
| **Owned destinations** | Site video location, canonical Method page, approved LinkedIn post, and any later Substack note that preserves the canonical link. |

## Script Architecture

| Time | Narrative job | Visual job | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Name the familiar confusion: public service language, sources, and buyer questions often sit apart. | Fragmented “claim / source / buyer question” cards move apart. | Do not assert a market-wide failure rate. |
| 0:12–0:32 | Explain why adding more pages, tools, or campaigns cannot by itself resolve an unclear decision. | A crowded pile becomes a route with one labeled decision. | Do not criticize competitors or claim channel ineffectiveness. |
| 0:32–0:58 | Show the method: identify the first constraint, check current support, connect the buyer question to a useful public route. | Three-step Signal Ledger diagram; source and limitation cards remain visible. | Describe a working method, not client outcomes. |
| 0:58–1:14 | Connect the method to SEO, content, paid media, and AI review as different routes that still need consistent public information. | Four connected route labels. | Do not claim visibility, ad performance, conversion, or AI behavior. |
| 1:14–1:30 | Invite the viewer to inspect the Method and select a next useful route. | Final Method-page card and textual CTA. | No urgency, form claim, or result promise. |

## Approved-Answer Chat Scope

The site chat path should be deterministic and local to approved public guidance. It may answer what the video explains, identify its stated limitations, and link to `/method`, `/topics`, the claim ledger, AI answer review, content refresh, or a conversation path. It must state when it does not have enough approved information.

| Visitor intent | Approved answer path | Must not say |
|---|---|---|
| “What is this video about?” | Summarize the reviewed script and offer `/method`. | “This will improve your rankings, visibility, or conversion.” |
| “How does this apply to SEO, content, or paid ads?” | Link to the corresponding public decision guide. | “We know your specific problem” or a service recommendation without a review. |
| “Will this make us show up in AI answers?” | Explain that the method improves inspectability of public information; link to AI answer review. | Any promise of AI citation, retrieval, control, or placement. |
| “Can I get a custom review?” | Offer the existing clarity-review conversation route. | A quoted scope, timing, result, or promise not already approved on the site. |

## Production and Review Gates

| Gate | Required evidence | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Script review** | Full narration, on-screen copy, cited public links, visual description, CTA, and no-guarantee check. | Research editor. |
| **Accessibility review** | Accurate captions, transcript, readable contrast, visual-label equivalents, and no audio-only critical instruction. | Accessibility/editorial reviewer. |
| **Claim review** | Every public claim is sourced, observable from the site, or explicitly framed as Coreweaver’s method. | Claim reviewer. |
| **Final edit review** | Frame consistency, citation/route accuracy, no fabricated proof, licensed audio/visual inputs, and end card behavior. | Video owner. |
| **Publication decision** | Human approval of the final rendered file, embed context, canonical page, transcript, and distribution copy. | Named publishing owner. |

## Measurement Without Outcome Claims

Measure the **operating health** of the video system: whether captions are present, the transcript matches the approved narration, the end CTA links to the correct canonical destination, and viewer questions can be answered by approved public sources. If a video host later provides analytics, record the source, definition, time window, and limitation before interpreting them. Do not set conversion, lead, revenue, audience-growth, or platform-performance targets from this system alone.

## Current Integration Validation

On August 20, 2026, the deterministic video-answer guide was visually reviewed within `/method` at desktop and mobile breakpoints. The guide remained in the Method reading path, displayed the approved-answer boundary before interaction, kept suggested prompts and the text field visible, and retained its warm Signal Ledger contrast and spacing. This validates the present interface only; it does not validate a future hosted video, transcript, captions, audio, distribution post, or viewer response.

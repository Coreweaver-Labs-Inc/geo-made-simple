# Coreweaver Labs Landing Page — Design Direction

## Three initial directions

### Theme Name: Signal Ledger
Very brief intro: A warm editorial system that makes complex AI visibility work feel concrete, legible, and considered. It uses paper, ink, brass, and restrained signal teal instead of a loud technology aesthetic.
Probability: 0.06

### Theme Name: Quiet Instrument
Very brief intro: A calm product-studio direction built around generous whitespace, instrument-like diagrams, and a sense of measured expertise. The page would feel more like a field guide than a typical SaaS homepage.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Dark Relay
Very brief intro: A high-contrast technical direction with deep graphite surfaces, luminous status markers, and a sharper systems feel. It preserves the reference site's seriousness while increasing the sense of live signal movement.
Probability: 0.08

## Chosen approach: Signal Ledger

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial modernism with influence from Swiss information design and independent research journals.

### Core Principles
1. Explain before impressing: every section answers one plain-language question.
2. Use contrast as hierarchy: warm paper, deep ink, brass for structure, and teal only for active signals.
3. Let content breathe: asymmetrical columns, generous margins, and short reading paths.
4. Make the abstract tangible: diagrams and editorial still lifes turn GEO concepts into things people can understand.

### Color Philosophy
The page begins with warm ivory rather than stark white so the brand feels human and grounded. Deep ink supplies authority, muted brass gives the system a considered, almost archival quality, and signal teal appears sparingly to mark action or live visibility. The palette is intentionally quieter than the reference so the copy does more work.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial rail establishes wayfinding while the content unfolds in a sequence of wide, asymmetric spreads. The hero uses a two-column split, while later sections alternate text-heavy panels with image-led moments. Avoid a stack of identical cards; every section should have a distinct reading rhythm.

### Signature Elements
- A small woven-knot mark that appears as both brand symbol and section marker.
- Thin brass rules and numbered labels that make the page feel like a field guide.
- Teal signal dots that indicate a system is active, measurable, or ready for action.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should clarify rather than entertain. Navigation highlights the user's current place, buttons give immediate tactile feedback, and the mobile menu is simple and direct. Motion is subtle: image drift, line reveals, and short fade-ups only where they support scanning.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, links, and cards. Let hero artwork move only a few pixels on hover. Use a gentle stagger for section labels and headings, but respect reduced-motion preferences. Avoid looping animations that compete with the message.

### Typography System
Use DM Sans for body copy and IBM Plex Mono for labels, navigation, and small metadata. Headlines are DM Sans with tight tracking and a strong weight contrast. Labels are uppercase, letter-spaced, and never longer than necessary.

### Brand Essence
Coreweaver Labs builds the practical systems that help brands become clear, credible, and citable in AI search. Personality: precise, grounded, quietly ambitious.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and declarative. CTAs name the next useful step rather than selling a vague transformation. Microcopy is calm, specific, and free of hype.

Example lines:
- "Make your brand easier for AI to understand."
- "Start with the signal you already have."

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact woven-knot symbol beside a text wordmark set in a clean, deliberate sans-serif. The symbol should be recognizable at favicon size and suggest systems that connect without becoming a literal network graphic.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Teal: #2E8B78. It is used as an ownable live-status color against ink and paper, not as a general accent everywhere.

## Content and SEO decisions

The page will use one clear H1, descriptive H2 sections, semantic HTML, descriptive internal anchor links, a title and meta description, canonical URL metadata, Open Graph metadata, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and Organization/WebSite JSON-LD. Copy will explain GEO and AI-answer visibility in plain language, while retaining relevant search phrases naturally.

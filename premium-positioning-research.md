# Premium Positioning Research — Working Notes

## Verified Findings

| Source | Finding | Positioning implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Edelman / LinkedIn 2024 B2B Thought Leadership Study](https://www.edelman.com/insights/thought-leadership-gets-b2b-buyers-back-into-game) | The report states that 73% of B2B buyers view thought leadership as a more trustworthy basis for judging competence than traditional marketing materials, and 60% of global B2B decision-makers and C-suite leaders say they would pay a premium for organizations that provide valuable thought leadership. | Premium positioning can credibly rest on original, useful, evidence-led expertise—not generic content volume or unsupported outcome promises. |
| [Content Marketing Institute 2025 B2B Benchmarks](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/b2b-research/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research-2025) | The benchmark frames high-performing B2B programs around strategy and planning, content creation and distribution, measurement and optimization, team structure, coordination, and governance of AI-supported output. | A premium content service should sell an integrated operating system: strategic planning, editorial quality, distribution, measurement, and governance—not isolated asset production. |

## Working Differentiation Hypotheses

The catalogue should position Coreweaver Labs as a premium partner only where the offering can be delivered and evidenced: senior strategic involvement, research-backed messaging, cross-channel coordination, transparent measurement, and governed claims/content. It should not promise rankings, lead volume, return on ad spend, or outcomes outside its control.

## Research Status

The next research pass should test the hypotheses against paid-media measurement and B2B buying-complexity sources, then convert only supportable conclusions into catalogue copy and pricing rationale.

# B2B Content Refresh — Public Resource Brief

**Status:** Approved evergreen-explainer brief. This record authorizes a bounded decision guide; it does not authorize a claim that a new date, a refresh, content volume, canonicalization, or a page change improves rankings or commercial outcomes.

## Source Contract

| Source-supported principle | Use in the guide | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|
| Google cautions against changing dates or adding/removing lots of content merely to appear fresh. | Explain why a refresh begins with a material buyer or source change, not a date or publication quota. | That updated material has no value, or that a particular refresh action will improve ranking. |
| Google encourages useful, original, people-first content with clear sourcing and substantial added value rather than copied or scaled material. | Ask whether an existing page still solves a distinct buyer decision with current support and a contribution beyond nearby pages. | That a checklist determines quality or success. |
| Google recommends reviewing and updating content as needed, or removing it if it is no longer relevant. | Include update, merge, qualify, retire, and develop as possible actions, subject to owner/source review. | That a page should be removed because it lacks immediate traffic or that removal produces a benefit. |
| Google describes canonicalization as a way to express a preferred URL for duplicate or similar content. | Treat canonical/redirect work as a technical follow-up after a content-purpose decision and relevant review. | That canonicalization is a substitute for a clear source, page purpose, or reader benefit. |

## Draft Shape

1. Frame the buyer question as “What should this page become?” rather than “How do we freshen it?”
2. Explain five bounded actions: update, merge, qualify, retire, or develop a distinct contribution.
3. Separate an observed change from a claim about results.
4. Connect the page’s purpose to current support, adjacent routes, and a named review owner.
5. Include a browser-local blank worksheet that neither submits nor stores inputs and tells readers not to add private material.
6. End with a no-guarantee boundary and a private next step.

## Publication Gate

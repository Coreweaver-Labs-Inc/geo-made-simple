# Coreweaver Labs — Market-Research Decision Register Template

**Use:** Complete one row for each source-bounded condition carried into the monthly private review. This template is an internal working record; it is not a public research output, a forecast, a commercial recommendation, or an authorization for external action.

| Field | Required entry | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Review date | ISO date and named review meeting. | Do not backdate a review to imply freshness. |
| Research lane | Market conditions, buyer/category, channel/platform, competitive context, or authority/content. | One record should answer one lane’s question. |
| Source contract | Publisher, URL, publication date, access date, source type, scope, and methodology or reporting limitation. | A source URL alone is not a complete contract. |
| Source-supported observation | One statement the source directly supports. | Do not mix the observation with Coreweaver’s opinion. |
| Coreweaver interpretation | A clearly labeled working interpretation. | Do not write this as a proven market condition. |
| Limitation | Sample, segment, geography, self-report, sponsorship, time period, access, or applicability boundary. | Missing limitations default to **hold**. |
| Decision | Hold, investigate, private content brief, improve public explanation, or defer. | No decision may publish, contact, spend, price, hire, or execute externally. |
| Accountable owner | Named person or role. | A task force record is incomplete without ownership. |
| Review trigger | Source update, policy change, service change, confirmed new evidence, or next review date. | “Monitor continuously” is not an acceptable trigger. |
| Editor disposition | Accept, request stronger support, archive, or defer. | A source-supported observation may still be declined as non-useful. |

## Monthly Review Agenda

| Segment | Intended result |
|---|---|
| Source check | Confirm that each source is accessible, current enough for its stated use, and described with its method and limitation. |
| Condition review | Separate source fact from Coreweaver interpretation; reject any record that turns a condition into a promise or unsupported comparison. |
| Decision routing | Select the smallest reversible next action. A content brief must retain its buyer question, parent silo, source plan, boundary, and human review gate. |
| Archive and renewal | Archive duplicates, expired sources, and records without a continuing decision need. Set review triggers for retained items. |

## Cadence Decision

The current default is a **monthly manual private review**. A weekly private digest is an optional future choice only after the owner selects a permitted source registry, a named reviewer, and an internal destination. Until then, no scheduled job, polling, public report, external notification, or automated action is authorized.

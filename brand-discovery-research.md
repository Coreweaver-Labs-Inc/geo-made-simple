# Brand Discovery Research Notes

**Status:** Working source record for the favicon, entity, AI-visibility, keyword-cluster, and channel-prioritization review. This file does not authorize external posting, profile creation, paid promotion, or changes to a third-party account.

## Audited Baseline

Coreweaver currently serves a single PNG favicon through the homepage head, uses the same mark as the Organization schema logo, exposes Organization and WebSite JSON-LD with four `sameAs` profiles (LinkedIn, Substack, X, and GitHub), and publishes an AI crawler policy that differentiates selected retrieval agents from selected model-training agents. The public site also has SSR, canonical URLs, a sitemap, author pages, research disclosures, AI Data Policy, and an evidence-led topic architecture.

## Technical Discovery Findings

Google allows a favicon in Search when the homepage exposes a stable icon URL, the home page and icon are crawlable, and the asset is a square that is at least 8×8px; Google recommends 48×48px or larger. [1] The current PNG link is valid in principle, but a self-hosted, stable multi-format icon set can make the implementation more resilient across browser, mobile, and search surfaces.

Google explains that Organization markup on a homepage can help it understand and disambiguate an organization. It recommends adding relevant properties only, keeping markup complete and accurate, and using a crawlable logo at least 112×112px. [2] The current Organization record already has the core identity, URL, description, logo, and verified `sameAs` URLs; it can be carefully extended only with facts that are public and verified.

Google’s current guidance for generative AI features reinforces foundational SEO, crawlability, unique helpful content, clear technical structure, and Search Console measurement. It specifically says that special AI files, keyword-variation content, inauthentic mentions, and special AI schema are not required. [3] Coreweaver’s AI-visibility work should therefore remain centered on accurate source-of-truth pages, documented expertise, internal links, and research quality—not an ‘AI visibility guarantee.’

## Channel Findings

Edelman and LinkedIn’s 2025 B2B thought-leadership report describes complex buying groups and positions strong thought leadership as a way to build trust and help internal stakeholders advocate for less familiar vendors. [4] This supports keeping LinkedIn as the primary professional distribution surface, while the site and Substack remain the canonical long-form sources.

Pinterest describes its business product as a discovery, planning, and shopping environment. [5] For Coreweaver, the fit is conditional: a business account is worth testing only when the site has durable visual assets such as methodology diagrams, editable worksheets, research cover art, or decision frameworks. It is not a default B2B posting channel.

Pinterest’s guidelines prohibit repetitive, deceptive, irrelevant, automated, or manipulated activity and require original, value-adding destinations. [6] The same no-automation and usefulness-first posture should govern any experimental external platform activity.

The Content Marketing Institute frames Reddit and Quora as communities where credibility and useful answers come before promotion, and warns that self-promotional behavior can be penalized. [7] For Coreweaver, both are **listening and named-expert participation** channels, not bulk link distribution surfaces. Any reply must be manual, directly responsive to the question, transparent about affiliation where relevant, and free of ranking/citation/outcome claims.

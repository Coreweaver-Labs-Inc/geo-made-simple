# Coreweaver Labs — Market-Research Task Force Charter

**Status:** Internal operating charter. This task force converts source-reviewed conditions and observations into private decision records. It does not make market forecasts, publish claims automatically, contact third parties, alter budgets, or authorize external actions.

## Purpose

The task force exists to help Coreweaver Labs move toward its evidence-led authority goal with better questions, current sources, and smaller accountable decisions. It watches conditions that may change how mid-market B2B teams interpret SEO, content, paid media, AI representation, and governance. It does not equate a trend with demand, a source with proof of an outcome, or market movement with a directive to publish.

## Research Lanes and Roles

| Lane | Research question | Accountable role | Permitted output | Cannot do alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Market conditions | What credible public sources say about budgets, buying caution, and channel context. | Market conditions researcher | Source note with scope and limitation. | Recommend a pricing, hiring, or investment decision. |
| Buyer and category | What questions, uncertainty, or due-diligence needs a mid-market B2B buyer may face. | Buyer-question researcher | Buyer-question proposal and public-information gap. | Generalize a vendor report into a universal buyer behavior claim. |
| Channel and platform | What official policies or public reports indicate about search, advertising, content, and AI-mediated discovery. | Platform researcher | Source-safe change note. | Promise visibility, retrieval, platform treatment, or performance. |
| Competitive context | What is visibly explained, missing, or differentiated in public category language. | Category analyst | Private comparison matrix with URLs and observation dates. | Make unverified performance, client, ranking, or intent assertions. |
| Authority and content | Which governed resource, Signal Note, guide, or public clarification might resolve a real information gap. | Content architect | Private resource brief with parent hub, links, source plan, and boundary. | Publish without named review. |
| Evidence and decision | Whether a record fairly separates observed fact, interpretation, limitation, and next action. | Evidence reviewer and strategy editor | Reviewed decision record. | Override the owner’s final public or commercial decision. |

## Evidence Standard

Every market record must distinguish **source fact**, **Coreweaver interpretation**, **limitation**, and **next decision**. Prefer official statistics, public regulatory or platform documentation, original research with disclosed methods, and transparent industry reports. Vendor research can inform a question but must retain its sample, self-report, sponsor, and generalizability limits.

> A market observation becomes a Coreweaver action only after a named reviewer records the source, scope, limitation, and smallest reversible next step.

| Record field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Observation | One concise statement that the cited source directly supports. |
| Source contract | URL, publisher, publication date, access date, methodology or reporting scope, and the source type. |
| Applicability | Why the observation may matter to mid-market B2B teams; explicitly identify what remains unknown. |
| Limitation | Sample, geography, segment, self-report, vendor sponsorship, time period, or access constraint. |
| Interpretation | A clearly labelled Coreweaver working view, never written as if it were source fact. |
| Decision | Hold, investigate, publish a private brief, improve a public explanation, or defer. |
| Owner and review trigger | Named role plus the evidence, date, service, or policy change that reopens the record. |

## Decision Routes

| Reviewed condition | Allowed next route | Gate before action |
|---|---|---|
| Stable source-supported condition | Private market note, service planning input, or next research question. | Evidence reviewer confirms the source contract. |
| Identified public-information gap | Private content brief or implemented child-guide proposal. | Content architect names buyer question, parent hub, related links, and a no-guarantee boundary. |
| Potential public claim or comparison | Hold in private claim ledger. | Source, method, named reviewer, and authorization when relevant. |
| Time-sensitive change | Draft-only Signal Note candidate. | Fresh primary source, limitation, named author, named reviewer, and editorial decision. |
| Price, staffing, contract, or external outreach implication | Owner decision record only. | Human owner approval; task force does not execute. |

## Monitoring Design Options

The task force should start with a manageable review loop. These are two viable operating approaches; neither is enabled by this charter.

| Approach | How it operates | Tradeoffs | Cost | Setup complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly source-review meeting** | Researchers collect a small, source-limited set of observations; a named editor reviews them in a monthly private session and records only accepted decisions. | Highest editorial control and the fewest false positives; slower to react to a genuinely time-sensitive change. | No recurring automation cost. | Low. |
| **Weekly private trend digest** | A scheduled internal job compiles permitted source links and creates one private draft record for review; no publication, outreach, or external action occurs. | Faster signal capture; requires clear source contracts, a reviewer, and maintenance of the approved-source list. | Ongoing system and model usage may apply depending on implementation. | Medium. |

The task force must not move to a recurring digest until the owner selects an approach, a review owner, the permitted source set, and the destination for private reports. An hourly or continuous polling loop is out of scope because it would add cost and noise without a stated decision need.

## First 90-Day Work

| Sequence | Focus | Decision artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Establish baseline conditions, source map, and SWOT. | Reviewed market-condition record and a limited source registry. |
| Month 2 | Test one priority information gap through a private content brief or public guide after review. | One approved content or research decision, not a volume target. |
| Month 3 | Review what was useful, what was uncertain, and which source contracts require renewal. | Keep, revise, or retire the task-force lanes and cadence. |

## Boundaries

- No automatic publishing, posting, emailing, lead generation, budget changes, pricing changes, or outreach.
- No fabricated competitor, buyer, client, market-share, or performance claims.
- No raw visitor, customer, prospect, private account, or unpublished research material in public records.
- No implication that monitoring, a SWOT, or a content action predicts or produces rankings, visibility, leads, pipeline, revenue, or return on ad spend.

# Coreweaver Labs — Launch Delivery Playbooks

**Audience:** Coreweaver Labs operators serving mid-market B2B teams.  
**Scope:** SEO, Content Marketing, and Paid Ads.  
**Operating rule:** These playbooks define a reviewable delivery system. They do **not** promise rankings, traffic, leads, revenue, conversion rate, or ROAS.

## Shared Delivery Standard

Every engagement begins with a written scope, named owner, access boundary, decision log, and success-measurement definition. Work is not treated as evidence merely because it was delivered. A public claim or case-study candidate needs its own authorized, sourceable record in the private governance workflow.

| Gate | Operator must record | Owner decision | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Buyer, scope, exclusions, systems, constraints | Approve the problem statement and access plan | Engagement brief |
| Baseline | Existing assets, date range, definitions, source locations | Confirm the measurement baseline | Baseline note |
| Work plan | Backlog, named work owner, dependencies, review dates | Prioritize and accept trade-offs | Delivery plan |
| Quality review | Evidence, factual checks, brand/legal/privacy concerns, unresolved limits | Approve, revise, or hold | Review record |
| Reporting | Completed work, decisions, sourceable observations, blocked items | Confirm next-quarter priorities | Decision-oriented report |

> **Boundary:** A dashboard observation is not a causal conclusion. Reports must distinguish the observation, the stated method, the plausible interpretation, and the decision it supports.

## SEO Playbook

| Stage | Primary work | Evidence and quality gate | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnose | Inventory technical, information-architecture, buyer-language, and evidence gaps | Document crawl/source date, scope, and exclusions | Signal audit |
| Design | Prioritize pages, structured information, internal routes, and maintenance responsibilities | Verify each proposed claim can be supported by a source or removed | Prioritized roadmap |
| Implement | Publish approved changes through the client’s normal release process | Record URLs, release date, reviewer, and rollback/contact owner | Release record |
| Learn | Compare the agreed observation period to the agreed baseline | Avoid inferring causality from a single movement or short time period | Review note |

**SEO quality questions:** Is the page useful to the intended buyer? Does it state who is responsible? Are important claims sourced, bounded, and current? Is the page technically reachable and indexable where appropriate? Is an update owner identified?

## Content Marketing Playbook

| Stage | Primary work | Evidence and quality gate | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question selection | Choose a buyer decision, uncertainty, or operating friction worth clarifying | Reject keyword-only briefs that lack reader value or an accountable question | Editorial brief |
| Research | Assemble approved internal evidence and Tier 1–3 external sources | Create a compact claim ledger: claim, evidence, scope, owner, decision | Source and claim ledger |
| Draft and review | Write the answer, limitations, method, author attribution, and next useful action | Named editorial/claims review before publication | Approved draft |
| Distribution and learning | Publish through approved channels and collect qualitative reader or sales/support feedback | Keep engagement observations separate from commercial attribution | Distribution note |

The standard follows Google’s people-first guidance: clear sourcing, visible authorship, original analysis where possible, and meaningful value beyond restatement.[1]

## Paid Ads Playbook

| Stage | Primary work | Evidence and quality gate | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Readiness | Confirm the buyer question, destination experience, tracking definitions, budget authority, and account permissions | Obtain written approval for spend, targeting constraints, and conversion definition | Campaign-readiness record |
| Message design | Translate approved buyer language into testable messages and destination alignment | Review claims, substantiation, jurisdiction, and platform-policy risks | Message matrix |
| Controlled test | Launch an approved, time-bounded test with documented audiences, budget, and change log | Do not expand spend without the designated approval | Test log |
| Decision review | Report delivery facts and relevant measurement context | Do not claim ROI, pipeline, or causal lift without a defined measurement method | Test review and next-decision memo |

## Cross-Functional Handoffs

| Handoff | Minimum information | Receiving owner |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing to sales | Buyer question, source asset, campaign or content context, requested follow-up | Sales owner |
| Marketing to support | Published explanation, expected customer confusion, policy boundary, escalation path | Support owner |
| Research to delivery | Sources, method, claim ledger, limitations, reviewer | Delivery owner |
| Delivery to governance | Candidate evidence, source-owner approval, reporting window, privacy/claims review, publication authorization | Governance reviewer |

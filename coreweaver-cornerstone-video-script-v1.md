# Coreweaver Labs — Cornerstone Video Script v1

**Working title:** *From unclear public information to a buyer-ready decision path*  
**Status:** Internal production draft. **Not recorded, generated, embedded, or published.** A named editor, claim reviewer, accessibility reviewer, and publishing owner must approve the final narration, visual copy, captions, audio, and destination before production release.

## Brief

| Field | Approved direction |
|---|---|
| Viewer | A mid-market B2B marketing, revenue, or operations lead assessing whether their public information supports a clear buyer decision. |
| Job | Orient the viewer to Coreweaver’s method and send them to one inspectable next route. |
| Master | 75–90 seconds, 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps. |
| Narration | Calm, clear, off-screen human or approved synthetic voice; no implied client experience. |
| Visual language | Warm paper, deep ink, brass rules, restrained teal signal; existing Method imagery plus simple original text and relationship cards. |
| Music | Restrained ambient instrumental, mixed beneath voice and removable without loss of meaning. |
| Primary CTA | “See the method” → `https://coreweaverlabs.com/method`. |
| Secondary public route | `https://coreweaverlabs.com/topics`. |

## Claim and Source Boundary

This video describes **Coreweaver’s public method**: one buyer question, current support, a clear limit, and a connected public route. It must not claim that the method improves a client’s rankings, traffic, AI appearance, citations, engagement, leads, pipeline, revenue, or return on ad spend. It must not show customer logos, dashboards, reviews, rankings, case outcomes, third-party interface simulations, or fabricated metrics.

The ending routes to the public Method because it documents the underlying decision gates and boundaries. Its practical claims are visible there rather than hidden in the video. [1]

## Visual and Audio Blueprint

| Layer | Direction | Production guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Existing imagery | Use the approved Method imagery: first constraint, evidence system, representation gap, and optional entity architecture. | Preserve source-file alt descriptions in the transcript/caption package; do not create a false impression that these images are client evidence. |
| Original motion graphics | Use typography, card relationships, brass rules, and restrained teal signal dots. | Motion should clarify sequence, not decorate claims. Avoid fabricated charts, metrics, product UI, or AI-result screens. |
| Typography | DM Sans for large statements; IBM Plex Mono for labels, route names, and source-boundary tags. | Maintain readable contrast and a minimum practical mobile-safe size in derivative layouts. |
| Narration | Neutral, paced, direct; no urgency, “guaranteed,” “proven,” or testimonial delivery. | Pronounce Coreweaver and every public route clearly; the final transcript is the approval source of truth. |
| Audio | Low, unintrusive ambient bed with gentle entrances and exits. | Music is not semantic. Captions and visual copy must carry all essential meaning. |

## Caption and Accessibility Package

The final video must ship with an edited **WebVTT** caption file, a complete transcript, and a public transcript region adjacent to the embed or on the same canonical route. Captions should be speaker-synchronized, sentence-faithful, and no more than two lines where feasible. They must include essential non-speech information only when it is needed to understand the experience, such as “[soft ambient music]” at the opening.

All route labels, boundary cards, and CTA text must also appear in narration or the transcript. The video should never require color alone to distinguish a source, a limitation, or a route.
